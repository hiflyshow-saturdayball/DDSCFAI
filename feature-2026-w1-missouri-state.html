No. 134 Over No. 13: Missouri State Outplays Texas A&M, 30-20

This was no fluke. Ken Lucero threw for 314 yards and 3 touchdowns, Missouri State converted 10 of 17 third downs and kept the ball for more than 37 minutes.

Some upsets need a bounce, a blocked kick or a late miracle. This one didn't. Missouri State came into Week 1 ranked No. 134. It beat No. 13 Texas A&M by 10, outgained Texas A&M 459 to 402, won the turnover battle 1-0 and never trailed after the middle of the third quarter. Texas A&M's only touchdown after the first quarter came with 1:33 left, down 17.

The Swing: Last Minute of the First Half

Texas A&M led 10-3 and was in control late in the second quarter. Then Ken Lucero found fullback Patrick Gaither for a 53-yard touchdown with 1:07 left. Robert Bain missed the extra point, so it was only 10-9, but the momentum had flipped. J. Lewis gave A&M a chance to answer with a 69-yard kickoff return, and Clarence Ellis missed from 46 yards with 23 seconds left. Halftime: 10-9, and Texas A&M came away empty.

Missouri State took the lead for good with 8:45 left in the third, when Lucero hit Rodney Andres for a 5-yard touchdown and the two-point conversion made it 17-10. A 6-yard scoring pass to Morton Travis 6 seconds into the fourth quarter made it 24-13, and Bain added field goals from 42 and 43 yards.

Missouri State had 24 first downs. Texas A&M had 12, and converted one third down all day.

Lucero: 26 of 35, No Mistakes

K. Lucero · 26/35 · 314 yds · 3 TD · 0 INT

Lucero completed 74 percent of his passes and spread them across the field. Gaither, a fullback, led everyone with 6 catches for 146 yards, including the 53-yard touchdown and a 29-yarder that flipped the field early in the fourth. Texas A&M sacked Lucero four times, twice each by defensive tackles Carmen Williams and Jeffrey Colburn, but never took the ball away from him.

The clincher was a grind. Leading 27-13 with 6:32 left, Missouri State drove from its own 20 to the Texas A&M 26 and used up four minutes, helped by a Lucero 32-yard completion to J. Lachance on 3rd-and-8 and an A&M personal foul on 3rd-and-5. Bain's 43-yard field goal made it 30-13 with 2:28 left.

Texas A&M: Big Plays, Then Nothing

J. Sprague · 15/23 · 310 yds · 1 TD · 0 INT

John Sprague's numbers look fine: 310 yards on 15 completions. But the yards came in chunks, not drives. Jeremy Nesmith caught a 76-yarder that set up his own 1-yard touchdown run in the first quarter and finished with 5 catches for 138 yards. Tyrell Merritt took a pass 54 yards to set up a field goal. Between the big plays, Texas A&M went 1 of 7 on third down, committed 8 penalties for 90 yards and lost a fumble at the Missouri State 28 on its second drive.

Safety Joe Hadley made 17 tackles, a sign of how often Missouri State got to the second level. Alexis Odum's 34-yard touchdown catch with 1:33 left made it 30-20, but A. Chandler recovered the onside kick for Missouri State, and three runs and a kneel-down later, it was over.
