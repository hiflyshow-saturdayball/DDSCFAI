Up 24, Holding On: No. 19 Tennessee Survives No. 23 Mississippi State, 34-31

Tennessee led 34-10 late in the third quarter. Mississippi State scored the last 21 points, converted two fourth downs and recovered an onside kick, but Tennessee fell on the second one to close out the SEC win.

Tennessee never trailed. For most of the night, it wasn't close. Then Mississippi State's Raul Springer completed 30 of 36 passes, his team scored 21 straight points and a 24-point Tennessee lead was down to three with 34 seconds to play. The final onside kick went to Tennessee, and James Mayes took a knee.

Tennessee Runs Away With It

E. Springer · 17 car · 141 yds · 1 TD

Tennessee ran for 226 yards on 35 carries, and Eugene Springer did most of the damage. His 35-yard run late in the first quarter set up Douglas Reynoso's 7-yard touchdown for a 10-0 lead, after Raymond McCants opened the scoring with a 30-yard field goal.

Mayes hit James McCall from 1 yard out early in the second quarter for 17-0. Mississippi State answered with Caleb Milne's 26-yard field goal, and on Tennessee's next snap Frank Ledesma sacked Mayes and forced a fumble, recovered by J. Bowlin. Springer found Aaron Ross for a 6-yard touchdown: 17-10. Tennessee punched back before the half. Facing third-and-7 at the Mississippi State 39 after a Paul Waters sack, Mayes threw a 39-yard touchdown to John Shultz with 26 seconds left: 24-10.

McCants added a 39-yard field goal in the third quarter. Joseph Gomez caught a 30-yard pass, and Eugene Springer went 25 yards for a touchdown with 3:59 left in the third. That made it 34-10.

The Swing at the Goal Line

Chris Perez's 2-yard touchdown catch cut it to 34-17 with 1:17 left in the third. Early in the fourth, Cornell Perry fumbled at the Mississippi State 6, L. Laughlin recovered and Tennessee had first-and-goal at the 1. Mayes threw for Clifton Hull, and Keith Batson intercepted it at the 1 and returned it 54 yards. Instead of 41-17, it stayed a 17-point game.

Tennessee had the ball at the Mississippi State 1 with 13:25 left and came away with nothing. It won by three.

The Rally Falls Short

R. Springer · 30/36 · 316 yds · 3 TD · 0 INT

From its own 15 with 6:59 left, Mississippi State had to convert twice on fourth down. Springer hit C. Green for 20 yards on fourth-and-17, then Robert Jacobsen for 48 yards on fourth-and-12 to the Tennessee 3. Anthony Jackson scored from 3 yards with 1:30 left: 34-24.

Jackson then recovered Milne's onside kick at the Tennessee 47. Springer completed five straight passes, the last a 9-yard touchdown to Glen Mock with 34 seconds left: 34-31. Milne tried another onside kick, Tennessee's S. Johnson fell on it, Mayes ran for a first down and Tennessee knelt it out.

Mississippi State sacked Mayes five times, two each by Waters and Ledesma, and won the turnover battle 2-1. It ran for 53 yards on 33 carries. Mayes finished 11 of 17 for 221 yards, with Gomez (3 for 73) and Hull (3 for 71) his top targets. Jacobsen led Mississippi State with 5 catches for 83 yards.
