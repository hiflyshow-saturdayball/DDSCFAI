Chisolm's 71-Yard Answer Lifts No. 20 Wisconsin Past No. 14 Texas A&M, 38-35

Stanley Chisolm broke the winning touchdown run 1:04 after Texas A&M had retaken the lead, and John Sprague's last seven passes fell incomplete. The lead changed hands eight times; Texas A&M falls to 0-2.

Eight lead changes, and the last one belonged to Stanley Chisolm. Texas A&M had just gone back in front 35-31 on a 29-yard touchdown pass with 9:29 to play. Chisolm answered with a 71-yard touchdown run at 8:25, and the Wisconsin defense took it from there. Texas A&M never scored again, and after a 30-20 loss at Missouri State in Week 1, the No. 14 team in the country is 0-2.

A Sloppy Start, Then a Slugfest

F. Huddleston · 20/26 · 271 yds · 2 TD · 0 INT

Texas A&M put the ball on the ground early: Charlie Goff fumbled at 13:11 of the first quarter, W. Bratcher recovered, and Jacob Wolfe's 32-yard field goal opened the scoring. Frankie Huddleston hit Antoine Beltran for a 26-yard touchdown at 2:45, and Wisconsin led 10-0.

Then came a 30-second giveaway swap. S. Hayes fumbled after a 9-yard catch and A. Alexander recovered for Texas A&M at 1:38; at 1:08, Walter Lange intercepted Sprague. Texas A&M's answer came in the second quarter, when Sprague hit Goff for 47 yards and Robert Downs scored from the 3 at 9:31. Carmen Williams sacked Huddleston on the next Wisconsin series, and Sprague's 2-yard touchdown pass to Harvey Cox with 31 seconds left in the half made it 14-10 Texas A&M. That 14-point run was the biggest of the day.

Trading Punches

J. Sprague · 21/34 · 229 yds · 3 TD · 1 INT

From there, nobody led by more than four. Bernard Greene's 3-yard run put Wisconsin up 17-14. Wilfredo Menendez answered with a strip-sack of Huddleston at 7:56 of the third quarter, and Sprague hit Jeremy Nesmith for a 10-yard score on the very next snap. Chisolm's 5-yard run made it 24-21; Downs' 6-yard run with 24 seconds left in the third made it 28-24 Texas A&M.

Fullback Ronny Kinard took a Huddleston pass 69 yards for a touchdown at 14:05 of the fourth. Sprague found Alexis Odum for 29 yards on second-and-3 at 9:29. Then Chisolm went 71 from his own 29, and Wisconsin had the last lead.

Chisolm finished with 118 yards on just 9 carries and two touchdowns.

The Finish

Texas A&M got to the Wisconsin 48 on its next drive, then threw three straight incompletions and punted from there at 4:23. Wisconsin started at its own 8 and William Beckham ran for a first down, but Williams stopped Chisolm and Beckham on second and third down, Texas A&M called timeouts after both, and Wisconsin punted on fourth-and-1 at 2:18.

Texas A&M took over at its own 38 with 2:11 left. G. Sharp broke up the first pass, a holding penalty pushed it back to second-and-20, and three more throws fell incomplete, the last on fourth-and-20 at 1:37. Sharp broke up three passes in the final 4:37. Wisconsin ran twice, picked up a first down and knelt it out.

Texas A&M had the edge in time of possession (32:05) and third downs (9 of 15), but committed eight penalties to Wisconsin's two. Wisconsin averaged 7.0 yards per carry and outgained Texas A&M 488-401.
