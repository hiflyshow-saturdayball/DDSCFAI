Missed From 52, Good From 49: No. 23 UCLA Edges No. 15 Clemson, 41-40

Steve Johnson missed with 2:08 left, then won it with 8 seconds to go after a fourth-down conversion and a Clemson personal foul. UCLA trailed by 11 three times and by 9 in the fourth quarter.

UCLA led this game for a total of 5 minutes and 29 seconds, and it was enough. Clemson scored on its first snap, led by 11 points three different times and was up 37-28 early in the fourth quarter. The Bruins kept coming: 572 yards, 439 of them through the air, and a kicker who got a second chance and made it count.

Clemson Comes Out Firing

J. Cooper · 19/31 · 332 yds · 3 TD · 1 INT

Jeremy Cooper's first pass went to Mitchell Mcallister for an 81-yard touchdown on Clemson's first snap. When S. Elam blocked a UCLA punt late in the first quarter, Cooper hit Andrew Williams from 8 yards out for a 14-3 lead. Mcallister caught a 14-yard touchdown in the second quarter (21-10) and Williams ran one in from 2 yards (28-17). Mcallister finished with 7 catches for 178 yards and two scores.

UCLA kept answering. James Corley's 2-yard touchdown pass to Vance Gatlin and Richard Chamberlin's 1-yard run kept it close, and Johnson's 34-yard field goal with 9 seconds left in the half made it 28-20.

The Comeback

Robby Rice ran in from 2 yards with 10:30 left in the third quarter and the two-point conversion was good: 28-28. Both teams then threw an interception, Lawrence Saunders picking off Cooper and Richard Acosta stopping a UCLA drive at the Clemson 4. Tony Davis' 33-yard field goal put Clemson back in front, and David Romo broke a 51-yard touchdown run with 12:57 left. Davis missed the extra point: 37-28, a miss that would decide the game.

Corley found Gatlin for 56 yards to set up Johnson's 31-yard field goal, and Chamberlin scored from the 1 with 6:38 left. Johnson's kick made it 38-37, UCLA's first lead of the night.

Clemson's missed extra point at 37-28 was the difference. Its last field goal put it up 40-38 instead of 41-38.

The Finish

J. Corley · 23/37 · 439 yds · 1 TD · 1 INT

UCLA tried to run out the clock, and Clemson burned all three of its timeouts on Chamberlin carries. Johnson's 52-yard try with 2:08 left was no good. Cooper hit Mcallister for 37 yards to reach the UCLA 13, two passes fell incomplete and Davis' 30-yard field goal made it 40-38 with 1:17 left.

The Bruins started at their own 27 and faced fourth-and-1 at the 36 with 21 seconds left. Corley found J. Hamer for 16 yards, and defensive end Matthew Durbin, who had two of Clemson's sacks, was flagged for unnecessary roughness. That put the ball at the Clemson 32, and Johnson made the 49-yarder with 8 seconds left. Cooper's final pass fell incomplete.

Corley was sacked six times but threw for 439 yards: Lawrence Gilmore caught 4 for 105, tight end Colin Dumas 5 for 101. Chamberlin ran 21 times for 82 yards and two touchdowns. For Clemson, Robert Patten carried 23 times for 101 yards.
