The New Top 10 · Week 2

Michigan Takes No. 1 in a Reshuffled Top 10

Two of the 10 teams are new to the top 10. Ohio State fell from No. 1 to No. 9.

By Saturday Desk staffWeek 2 · 2026 season

Two of the 10 teams in the new top 10 were not there a week ago: USC (from No. 11) and Oklahoma (from No. 12). Out are Florida State (No. 4 to No. 11) and Alabama (No. 5 to No. 19).

On the field, 9 of the 10 won, none lost and 1 had a bye. The closest call was Texas's 34-30 win at No. 18 Oregon. The biggest margin belonged to Notre Dame, 46-12 over SMU.

Our Desk poll, built from results and rosters, has the same rank as the game for 2 of the 12 teams that sit in either top 10 (Texas and Penn State). The widest splits: Michigan (game No. 1, Desk No. 12); Florida State (game No. 11, Desk No. 4); Miami (FL) (game No. 13, Desk No. 6).
