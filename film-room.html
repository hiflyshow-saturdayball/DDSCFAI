Fischer's Film Room · Week 2

Fischer Got His Clock Back. Now He Wants the Percentage.

Kansas State's quarterbacks coach on a 30-3 win, a red-zone interception he'd call again and the Big 12 opener against Arizona State.

Jose Fischer, Quarterbacks Coach, Kansas StateAs told to Saturday Desk · Week 2 · 2026 season

A week ago, Jose Fischer said his offense had to stay on the field. It did. No. 27 Kansas State beat Southern Miss 30-3 in Manhattan, held the ball for 36:26 after managing only 22:11 against Air Force, and outgained Southern Miss 505 yards to 261. Anthony Rice threw three touchdown passes. He also completed 13 of 23, the same 56 percent he hit in Week 1. Kansas State's quarterbacks coach sat down with the Desk for the second edition of the Film Room.

You asked for the clock back and got it: 36:26 of possession and 505 yards. But Rice completed 13 of 23 again, about 56 percent, the same as Week 1. Was this the game you asked for, and what's still missing?

We saw some improvements, but there is still hard work to do.

The improvements are easy to find. Kansas State ran up 24 first downs to Southern Miss's 12, and the ground game carried its share: Ricky Wheat ran 14 times for 99 yards and Jonathan Vasquez 10 times for 67. Rice threw for 215 yards, hitting Charles Palacios from 12 yards, Joe Strickland from 3 and redshirt freshman Thomas Fenner from 12. The hard work is on third down, where Kansas State converted 4 of 9, and in the completion column, which hasn't moved.

Up 20-3 early in the third quarter, on second-and-2 from the Southern Miss 6, Rice threw for Vasquez and D. Macon intercepted him at the 1. Was that a bad decision or a bad throw, and how do you coach him through it?

I'd love to see him aggressive and pushing the ball. Sometimes it doesn't work out, but we'll keep calling passes in those situations.

The coach isn't backing off the call. The interception was Rice's only one of the day, and Kansas State didn't give up a point after it: Southern Miss's only score was a 36-yard field goal before halftime. Rice went right back to pushing the ball, with completions of 30 yards to Strickland and 22 to V. Chapa later in the third quarter, and Kansas State added a John Arroyo field goal and Fenner's touchdown in the fourth.

Arizona State comes to Manhattan for Kansas State's Big 12 opener. Arizona State scores 27 points a game, Kansas State allows 15.5, and our line has Kansas State by 7. What does Rice need to do?

Keep playing with confidence, and hopefully complete a higher percentage of passes.

Arizona State is 1-1 and No. 81 in the poll. It lost 44-21 at home to Western Kentucky in Week 1, then held off Maryland 33-30. Rice is 27 of 48 for 581 yards with six touchdowns and two interceptions through two games. The yards are there. The completions are the next step.

Kansas State (2-0) hosts Arizona State in Week 3. The Film Room returns next week.
