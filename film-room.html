Fischer's Film Room · Week 1

Rice Threw for 366. Fischer Wants the Clock Back.

Kansas State's quarterbacks coach on a 56 percent day, a deep room and a Southern Miss team with something to prove.

Jose Fischer, Quarterbacks Coach, Kansas StateAs told to Saturday Desk · Week 1 · 2026 season

Anthony Rice put up 366 yards and three touchdowns against Air Force, and the man who coaches him still found the part of the tape he didn't like. No. 26 Kansas State beat the Falcons 38-28, but Air Force held the ball for 37 minutes and 49 seconds to the Wildcats' 22:11, and the game was tied at 28 with 12:20 left. Jose Fischer, Kansas State's quarterbacks coach, sat down with the Desk for the first edition of the Film Room.

Rice went 14 of 25 for 366 yards and three touchdowns. What did you see on the tape that the box score doesn't show, and what's your note to him this week?

Our completion percentage needs to improve, and our offense needs to stay on the field more.

The numbers back the coach up. Rice completed 56 percent of his throws, and Kansas State was 5 of 11 on third down against Air Force's 9 of 16. The Falcons ran 21 first downs to the Wildcats' 12. Rice's touchdown passes covered 38, 19 and 2 yards, big plays that hid how little time the offense spent on the field. Kansas State needed a 26-yard field goal and a 26-yard interception return by Landon Grice with 17 seconds left to put it away.

Michael Little and Justin Davison are both four-star sophomores rated 84, ten points behind Rice. How do you sell that depth to the room?

Competition is good for the team. The season is long and hard.

Rice, a senior rated 94, is the starter. Behind him sit Little and Davison, then freshman Miguel Stock at 79 and sophomore Eddie Sturgill at 77, who gained a point in training camp. That's five quarterbacks, three of them four-star recruits, for a season that will test every one of them.

Southern Miss comes to Manhattan in Week 2, ranked No. 115. What do you tell the room about not looking past it?

Southern Miss put on a great battle against Vanderbilt, and the game was close until late. They'll be ready to fight, and they're looking for their first win of the season.

The tape agrees. Southern Miss led Vanderbilt 28-27 at the half and trailed only 38-35 with 9:17 left before the Commodores pulled away 45-35. The Golden Eagles are 0-1 and ranked No. 115, and they arrive in Manhattan with every reason to swing.

Kansas State (1-0) hosts Southern Miss in Week 2. The Film Room returns next week.
