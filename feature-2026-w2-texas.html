Horsley Goes 91: No. 7 Texas Rallies From 20 Down to Stun No. 18 Oregon, 34-30

Texas trailed 23-3 in the third quarter, scored 28 points in the fourth and took its only lead on Roberto Horsley's punt-return touchdown with 33 seconds left. Rey Carter had four of the Texas defense's 10 sacks.

Texas led this game for 33 seconds, and it was enough. Shut out in the first half and down 23-3 midway through the third quarter, Texas scored 28 points in the fourth, and the last seven came from Roberto Horsley, who took an Oregon punt 91 yards for the winning touchdown with 0:33 left.

Oregon in Control

D. Vega · 15/30 · 369 yds · 2 TD · 1 INT

For a half, it was all Oregon. Gary Cavanaugh was sacked seven times before the break, Texas lost a fumble at its own 30 with 13:14 left in the first quarter and another on an Anthony Garcia strip-sack, and the Texas offense punted six times. Mark Peck kicked field goals of 33 and 28 yards, Randall Crouch scored from a yard out after a 49-yard catch by Keytaon Schaefer, and David Gantt's 42-yard grab with 26 seconds left set up Peck's 18-yarder: 16-0 at the half.

Thomas Crowe opened the second half with a 49-yard catch, but Texas settled for James Frizzell's 41-yard field goal. Oregon answered with a 29-yard touchdown pass from David Vega to Randell Limon, and it was 23-3 with 10:21 left in the third. Charles Askew intercepted Vega later in the quarter, and Frizzell's 26-yarder made it 23-6 heading to the fourth.

Two Strips, 99 Seconds

G. Cavanaugh · 16/28 · 268 yds · 2 TD · 0 INT

The game flipped on back-to-back Oregon possessions. With 10:56 left, Richard Dinkins sacked Vega and recovered the fumble, and on the next snap Cavanaugh hit Rosendo Knight for a 29-yard touchdown. On Oregon's next series, Carter sacked Vega twice in a row, forcing a fumble on the second and recovering it himself at the Oregon 12. Knight ran in from 5 yards at 9:06. Two touchdowns in 99 seconds, and it was 23-20.

Oregon had an answer. Vega found Phillip Dove for a 52-yard touchdown at 6:02 to push the lead back to 30-20. Texas answered right back: Cavanaugh connected with Kemon Troutman for 57 yards, and Peter Olson caught an 8-yard touchdown pass with 2:49 left. 30-27.

Texas was outgained 487 to 373 and managed 10 first downs to Oregon's 15. It won the turnover battle 3-2 and turned the two fourth-quarter strip-sacks into 14 points.

The Finish

Oregon took over at its own 33 and ran three straight times, with Schaefer's 14-yard carry moving the chains, and Texas called a timeout after each one. Then the Texas front took over: Carter sacked Vega with 2:03 left, his fourth of the day, and Dale Rios sacked him on third down at 1:14, leaving Oregon with fourth-and-21 at its own 39.

A. Baker's punt went 52 yards. Horsley took it down the left side and went the distance. Frizzell's kick made it 34-30. Vega hit Schaefer for 14 yards on the final drive, but his last pass fell incomplete.

Knight finished with 53 yards on 15 carries plus his touchdown catch, and Crowe caught 4 passes for 114 yards. For Oregon, Gantt caught 5 for 153 and Dove ran 12 times for 62 yards. Garcia had three of Oregon's nine sacks. Kenneth Aguirre led Texas with 11 tackles.
