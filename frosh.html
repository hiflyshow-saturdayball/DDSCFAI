WHO COUNTS

  • True freshmen (Fr)First season of college football.
  • Redshirt freshmen (RS Fr)Sat out a season, now playing year one. The game uses the same rule.
  • Sophomores (So, RS So)Year two on the field, with or without a redshirt season. Shown in their own board, not in the freshman race.

HOW THE DESK RANKS

  • Same formula as the Heisman WatchSo a freshman's points mean the same thing on both boards.
  • The detailsProduction: 1 pt per 25 pass yds, 4 per pass TD, -3 per INT, 1 per 10 rush/rec yds, 6 per rush/rec TD, -2 per fumble lost. Defense (x0.6): 4 per sack, 5 per INT, 1.5 per TFL, 3 per FF, 6 per TD, 0.3 per tackle. Team: +4 per win, -3 per loss; top 10 x1.20, top 25 x1.10, outside top 50 x0.85.
  • No official awardThe game doesn't hand out a freshman award. This board is the Desk's own.
