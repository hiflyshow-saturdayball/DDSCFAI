HOW THE GAME PICKS

  • Regular season onlyBowl and playoff games don't count.
  • Stats, not winsBig individual numbers drive the race, and defensive plays like sacks count for a lot. Winning barely moves the needle.
  • A small bump for the elitePlaying for a top-10 team helps a little.
  • Three finalistsThe top 3 go to New York, and the frontrunner doesn't always win.

HOW THE DESK RANKS

  • Production1 pt per 25 pass yds, 4 per pass TD, −3 per INT, 1 per 10 rush or rec yds, 6 per rush or rec TD.
  • Defense at 60%Sacks, picks, TFLs and forced fumbles count, discounted the way voters discount them.
  • Winning matters+4 per win, −3 per loss. Top 10 ×1.20, top 25 ×1.10, outside the top 50 ×0.85.
