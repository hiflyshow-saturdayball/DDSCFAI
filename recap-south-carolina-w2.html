Twenty Straight: No. 50 South Carolina Takes Down No. 26 Pittsburgh, 27-20

Robert Westbrook threw three touchdown passes, Khaleke Patterson's strip-sack flipped the third quarter and George Weber's 17-yard run on third-and-5 slammed the door after Pittsburgh pulled within a touchdown.

No. 26 Pittsburgh arrived off a 45-14 rout of Connecticut. No. 50 South Carolina arrived off a loss at Colorado. For 12 minutes and 49 seconds of the second quarter, the script held. Then South Carolina rattled off 20 unanswered points, never trailed again and held on, 27-20, despite being outgained 353 yards to 349.

The Run

R. Westbrook · 15/20 · 178 yds · 3 TD · 0 INT

After a scoreless first quarter, Benjamin Granger hit Travis Carrera for a 23-yard touchdown on the first snap of the second, and Pittsburgh led 7-0. Brooks Stuckey's 40-yard field goal at 9:19 got South Carolina on the board, and with 1:11 left in the half Westbrook found Jamey Millard for a 32-yard touchdown: 10-7, the game's only lead change.

Pittsburgh had a chance to answer before the break, but Mark Levine's 54-yard field goal try with 20 seconds left was no good.

The Turning Point

Midway through the third quarter, Pittsburgh faced third-and-3 at its own 27. Linebacker Khaleke Patterson came through, sacked Granger for a 7-yard loss, jarred the ball loose and recovered it himself, the game's only turnover. It was Patterson's second strip-sack of the day; Pittsburgh had recovered the first one in the opening quarter. South Carolina's drive stalled at the Pittsburgh 12, and Stuckey's 29-yarder made it 13-7.

Early in the fourth quarter, Westbrook converted third-and-3 from the 8 with a touchdown pass to fullback Nickolas Shepherd. That made it 20-7 with 13:30 to play.

Pittsburgh scored on its first possession of the second quarter, then went until 6:43 of the fourth without another point.

The Finish

B. Granger · 23/28 · 284 yds · 2 TD · 0 INT

Robert Reese's 4-yard run finally broke the drought with 6:43 left, but Levine's extra point was no good: 20-13. South Carolina answered with a 75-yard drive from its own 25. Westbrook hit B. Rapp for 24, Shepherd for 18 and Weber for 16, and Millard caught his second touchdown, a 6-yarder with 3:46 left, for a 27-13 lead, South Carolina's largest of the game.

Granger kept Pittsburgh alive. Starting from its own 31 after M. Frias' 27-yard kickoff return, Pittsburgh went 69 yards in 1:59, and Granger capped it with an 18-yard touchdown pass to Dawuane Whitley with 1:33 left. Levine's kick made it 27-20.

Pittsburgh never saw the ball again. It called timeout after each of South Carolina's first three snaps, and on third-and-5 from the South Carolina 16, Weber ran for 17 yards and a first down. South Carolina knelt twice and it was over.

South Carolina ran 44 times for 171 yards, with Mitchell Mabry (12 for 66) and Weber (14 for 60) sharing the work, and held a 32:41 to 27:19 edge in possession. Millard finished with 5 catches for 53 yards and two scores. Granger completed 23 of 28 passes for 284 yards, with Whitley catching 6 for 86 and tight end Russell Inman 6 for 62, but Pittsburgh managed only 69 rushing yards on 27 carries.
