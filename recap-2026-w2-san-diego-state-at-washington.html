No. 17 Washington held on for a 38-35 win over visiting San Diego State. Michael Spalding stole the show with 2 interceptions. San Diego State led 21-14 at halftime.

The decisive stretch: Washington scored 17 straight points from 2:11 of the third quarter to 1:04 of the fourth. Washington came back from 14 points down. The lead changed hands 4 times. Trailing 35-31, Washington won it on a 51-yard touchdown pass from Demetrius Emerson to Larry Bergeron with 0:22 left.

Demetrius Emerson went 17-of-26 for 277 yards, 4 touchdowns and an interception for Washington. Gregory Jenson ran 12 times for 57 yards. Larry Bergeron caught 5 passes for 109 yards and 2 touchdowns. On defense, Michael Spalding had 3 tackles and 2 interceptions.

For San Diego State, Joel Catron went 13-of-23 for 140 yards, 2 touchdowns and 2 interceptions. San Diego State actually outgained Washington 469-414. Turnovers: Washington 2, San Diego State 2.
