Dual Threat · Week 2

Beckett Runs the Show: The Week 2 Dual-Threat Power List

Three rushing touchdowns in Chestnut Hill, a 24-carry grind in Tempe and four scores in Kalamazoo.

By Saturday Desk staffWeek 2 · 2026 season

Seven quarterbacks cleared the bar this week, and only three of them walked off winners. Those three also top the list. Kenneth Beckett went from honorable mention to No. 1, and last week's No. 1, Donald Coombs, slid after a long night at No. 12 Oklahoma.

1. K. Beckett, Virginia

18/21 · 175 yds · 0 TD · 0 INT  |  22 car · 136 yds · 3 TD
Virginia 47, Boston College 27 · on the road

Beckett didn't need to throw a touchdown pass. He completed 86 percent of his throws and scored three times himself, on runs of 4, 6 and 2 yards. The 6-yarder came with 32 seconds left in the first half and made it 23-10. The last one, with 1:24 to go, ended any thought of a Boston College rally. That's 311 yards of total offense, and he's now on this list two weeks running.

Eighteen of 21 through the air and three trips to the end zone on the ground. Pick your poison, and you're still wrong.

2. L. Martin, Arizona State

7/17 · 90 yds · 2 TD · 0 INT  |  24 car · 107 yds
Arizona State 33, Maryland 30

It wasn't pretty through the air, but Martin carried the Sun Devils for three quarters: 24 carries for 107 yards, plus touchdown throws of 27 and 9 yards to William Meehan that built a 23-13 lead. After a field goal made it 26-13, Maryland scored 17 straight. The winner came from Michael Curry, a 6-yard pass to Philip Hernandez with 16 seconds left.

3. J. Mcdaniel, Western Michigan

20/23 · 205 yds · 3 TD · 1 INT  |  19 car · 82 yds · 1 TD
Western Michigan 56, Michigan State 27

Four touchdowns in the first half: three passes and an 8-yard run, for a 28-10 lead at the break. He gave a few back, with an interception and two lost fumbles, two of those three in the opening minute of the fourth. It didn't matter. The Broncos scored the last three touchdowns and won by 29.

Honorable mentions

  • K. Miller, Florida Atlantic: 231 passing yards, 78 rushing and three total touchdowns (two runs, one pass) in a 36-21 loss at Louisville. His 10-yard run made it 26-21 late in the third before the Cardinals pulled away. Tied with Mcdaniel on the index; the win broke the tie. Recap →
  • D. Coombs, Buffalo: Last week's No. 1 carried 23 times for 86 yards and threw for 110 in a 43-9 loss at No. 12 Oklahoma. Sixty-six carries in two weeks is a lot to ask of a quarterback. Recap →

Criteria: at least 10 pass attempts and 50 rushing yards. Ranked on the dual-threat index: total yards and touchdowns, weighted by the opponent's pre-game rank, minus turnovers, plus a bonus for a win (same index as Weekly Tops).
