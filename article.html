Legs, Arms, Chaos: The Week 1 Dual-Threat Power List

A Buffalo upset, a stand against No. 1 and a shootout in Atlanta.

By Saturday Desk staffWeek 1 · 2026 season

Opening weekend belonged to the quarterbacks who refused to pick a lane. Three of them threw for more than 180 yards and ran for more than 90, and one of them took down a Power Four program on the road.

1. D. Coombs, Buffalo

10/20 · 186 yds · 2 TD · 0 INT  |  20 car · 143 yds · 1 TD
Buffalo 37, No. 75 Duke 23

The Bulls arrived as a 52-spot underdog in the rankings and left with a statement. Coombs was the whole offense: 329 yards of total offense, three touchdowns, and the 25-yard run that sealed it at 4:35 of the fourth.

Twenty carries from your quarterback is a workload. Twenty carries in an upset win is a résumé.

2. J. Herron, South Alabama

16/19 · 199 yds · 1 TD · 0 INT  |  23 car · 137 yds
No. 1 Ohio State 37, South Alabama 16

The scoreboard says loss. The tape says Ohio State spent four quarters chasing this guy: 84 percent passing and 336 total yards against the No. 1 team in the country.

3. K. Miller, Florida Atlantic

16/22 · 242 yds · 1 TD · 0 INT  |  14 car · 94 yds · 1 TD
No. 36 Georgia Tech 44, Florida Atlantic 30

Clean, efficient and dangerous: 336 total yards, a touchdown both ways, and no turnovers.

Honorable mentions

  • K. Beckett, Virginia: 4 passing TD and 100 rushing yards in a 55-24 rout of Indiana.
  • L. Martin, Arizona State: 119 rushing yards and 2 TD in a loss to Western Kentucky.

Criteria: at least 10 pass attempts and 50 rushing yards; ranked on total yards, touchdowns and turnovers, weighted by result and opponent.
