HOW THE DESK RANKS

  • ScopeNo earlier snapshot is archived yet, so this board is season-to-date. From next week it switches to the week alone.
  • PointsProduction: 1 pt per 25 pass yds, 4 per pass TD, -3 per INT, 1 per 10 rush/rec yds, 6 per rush/rec TD, -2 per fumble lost. Defense (x0.6): 4 per sack, 5 per INT, 1.5 per TFL, 3 per FF, 6 per TD, 0.3 per tackle. Team: +4 per win, -3 per loss; top 10 x1.20, top 25 x1.10, outside top 50 x0.85.
  • Dual-threat barSame bar as the Dual Threat column: 10+ pass attempts and 50+ rush yards. Index = (1 per 25 total yards + 4 per TD) x opponent weight - 3 per turnover + 4 for a win. Opponent weight by the other team's pre-game rank: top 10 x1.20, 11-25 x1.10, 26-50 x1.00, above 50 x0.85 (x1.00 if the opponent is unknown).
