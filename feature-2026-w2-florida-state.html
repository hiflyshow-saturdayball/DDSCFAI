Fourth-and-9, Then Henke: No. 4 Florida State Steals One at No. 16 Auburn, 27-23

Florida State led for only the final 29 seconds. John Betancourt converted a fourth-and-9 from his own 20 and found Edwin Henke for the winning 18-yard touchdown after Auburn outgained the visitors 543-423.

Auburn ran for 334 yards, held the ball for 35:46 and never trailed until there were 29 seconds left. Florida State was down 10 in the second quarter and down three with five minutes to play. Then John Betancourt took over: an 80-yard drive, a fourth-and-9 conversion from his own 20 and an 18-yard strike to Edwin Henke that won it.

Auburn Moves It, Florida State Hangs Around

D. Flores · 18/26 · 209 yds · 0 TD · 1 INT

Auburn kept getting close and kept settling. Julian Blunt kicked field goals from the Florida State 7 and 14, and on the drive in between Dexter Flores threw for J. Yanez from the 5 with 4:12 left in the half. Free safety Dylan Malley picked it off at the 1 and returned it 16 yards, keeping it 6-3.

Auburn found the end zone anyway. Luke Marcum broke a 60-yard run on third-and-5, and receiver Robert Snyder scored on a 1-yard run with 31 seconds left: 13-3, Auburn's largest lead. Florida State needed two plays to answer. Betancourt hit Henke for 29 yards, a taunting penalty on Auburn's F. Preston tacked on more, and Howard Clary made a 47-yard field goal with 15 seconds left to make it 13-6 at the half.

Trading Punches in the Third

Adam Utley tied it with a 4-yard touchdown run at 8:55. Auburn answered in two minutes: J. Campbell returned the kickoff 36 yards, Augustus Tripp ripped off a 51-yard run and Marcum scored from 13 out for a 20-13 lead. Florida State's reply was even quicker. William Rodriguez brought the kickoff back 40 yards and Kermit Ramos ran 33 yards for a touchdown at 4:45. 20-20.

Blunt's 45-yard try with 1:16 left in the quarter was no good. Marcum finished with 173 yards on 23 carries, Tripp added 120 on 16.

Auburn kicked field goals from the Florida State 7, 14 and 3 and threw an interception from the 5. It piled up 543 yards and scored 23 points.

Fourth-and-9

J. Betancourt · 17/22 · 236 yds · 1 TD · 0 INT

Auburn reached first-and-goal at the Florida State 9 early in the fourth. Marcum ran three straight times for 5 yards, linebacker Mario McCray making two of the stops, and Blunt's 20-yard field goal made it 23-20 with 5:00 left.

Florida State's next drive ended when Edward Overby sacked Betancourt and forced a fumble that Harland Paige recovered. Auburn took over at the Florida State 33 with 3:17 left, but the Florida State defense allowed minus-1 yard on three plays, F. McClain breaking up the only pass, and Auburn punted for a touchback.

Florida State took over at its 20 with 2:34 left. Kevin Oliva sacked Betancourt for 9 yards, leaving fourth-and-9 at the 20. Betancourt found Henke for 13 yards and a first down. G. Fleming caught one for 12, Betancourt ran for 18 and Parker Fleming caught an 18-yarder to the Auburn 18. After a spike at 0:30, Betancourt hit Henke in the end zone. Clary's kick made it 27-23.

Auburn had one more shot. Snyder caught a 29-yard pass on the final play, but time ran out at the Florida State 41. Henke finished with 7 catches for 111 yards, Snyder 7 for 102. McCray had 12 tackles.
