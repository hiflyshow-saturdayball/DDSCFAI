It took overtime. Cincinnati beat North Dakota State 70-69, 139 combined points. The headline number: 374 passing yards and 6 touchdowns for Joshua Cody. Cincinnati led 35-24 at halftime.

The decisive stretch: Cincinnati scored 21 straight points in the first quarter, from 13:02 to 4:55. The lead changed hands twice. Trailing 69-63, Cincinnati won it on a 5-yard touchdown pass from Joshua Cody to Frank Mckinney in overtime.

Joshua Cody went 25-of-33 for 374 yards with 6 touchdowns for Cincinnati. Ismael Stiltner ran 16 times for 162 yards and a score. Carl Briscoe caught 5 passes for 152 yards and 3 touchdowns. On defense, Timothy Dayton had 12 tackles.

For North Dakota State, William Zook went 16-of-28 for 369 yards with 5 touchdowns. North Dakota State actually outgained Cincinnati 705-636. Turnovers: Cincinnati 1, North Dakota State 0.
