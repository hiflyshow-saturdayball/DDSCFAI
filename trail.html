No. 10 Darrell Jeter, LB ★★★★★ · Ford City, PA

NEW · Week 2 report

Consistently takes down ball carrier on tackles. Not the strongest, but strong enough. Can maintain his leverage and shed blocks. More than enough speed for the position. This will become evident as he develops. The young Jeter has his phone ringing off the hook. Darrell Jeter recently told local news that the following teams have shown interest: Pittsburgh Panthers, West Virginia Mountaineers, Penn State Nittany Lions, Michigan Wolverines. No one knows where Darrell Jeter will play next year. He has been mum on the subject - even with his close relatives.

His good natured behavior, even toward opponents have earned him some criticism, but also an awful lot of respect. He studies hard. Incredible accuracy on this kid. Incredible arm strength lets him deliver the ball however you want.

No. 2 Walter Nakamura, G ★★★★★ · Forrest City, AR

Week 1 report

Not afraid to lead. Due to his work ethic, this kid is very coachable. Respected for his good behavior on the field. With the amount this youngster can already benchpres, he will only ever have to focus on improving technique. Nice blocking skills on run plays. He will contribute to the running game. Consistently makes good decisions on the field.

Who has called, per the report: Ole Miss Rebels, Memphis Tigers

Our board: Leader: Ole Miss 20 · then Memphis 6

No. 5 Jarrod Prescott, WR ★★★★★ · San Antonio, TX

Week 1 report

His high school coaches rave about his work ethic. This is the kind of kid you want to promote the game. Well behaved, loves the game, and respectful. He will never throw someone else under the bus to save himself. His college tape shows him catching the ball well. Fast enough to challenge good cornerbacks in man coverage. And enough speed to threaten the deep ball. Adjusts well to incoming tacklers, and can use his agility and balance to turn a short catch into a big play.

Who has called, per the report: SMU Mustangs, Texas A&M Aggies, Oregon Ducks, Washington Huskies, UTEP Miners, Texas Longhorns, Sam Houston State Bearkats

Our board: Leader: Washington 22 · then Texas A&M 21, Oregon 17

HOW THE TRAIL WORKS

  • InterestHow much a prospect likes a school, on a scale up to 100. It grows when the school works for him: calls, visits, NIL, promises.
  • CommittingA prospect needs a scholarship offer to commit. The school with the most interest is the favorite, not a lock: close races can go either way.
  • Signing daysEarly Signing Day in February, then three spring recruiting periods and the Final Signing Period in July.
