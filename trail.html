HOW THE TRAIL WORKS

  • InterestHow much a prospect likes a school, on a scale up to 100. It grows when the school works for him: calls, visits, NIL, promises.
  • CommittingA prospect needs a scholarship offer to commit. The school with the most interest is the favorite, not a lock: close races can go either way.
  • Signing daysEarly Signing Day in February, then three spring recruiting periods and the Final Signing Period in July.
