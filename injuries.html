HOW TO READ IT

  • StatusThe league's own label, as it appears in the game. Recovery times are estimates.
  • Game-time decisionsDoubtful, questionable and probable players may still play.
  • HurtThe week the player first showed up on the report, tracked from the weekly snapshots. NEW = this week. "game" = the play-by-play logs the injury in that week's game; otherwise it happened outside a game (practice). "Preseason" = already hurt when tracking began (Week 1 report), not tied to a Week 1 game.
