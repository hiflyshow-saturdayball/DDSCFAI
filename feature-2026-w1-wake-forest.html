No Good From 50: Wake Forest Survives No. 5 Alabama, 29-26

Three turnovers, a blown 11-point lead and one last Alabama drive. Then the 50-yard kick to tie it missed with 58 seconds left.

Wake Forest did just about everything it could to give this one back. It fumbled twice and threw an interception. It let a 14-3 lead disappear in a 16-point Alabama burst. With 2:26 left, in Alabama territory with a chance to put it away, it put the ball on the ground at the Alabama 36. And it still won, because William Tovar's 50-yard try to tie the game missed with 58 seconds on the clock.

No. 73 over No. 5. On opening weekend. That's the biggest upset on the Week 1 board, and nothing else is close.

The Ending: Two Minutes, One Kick

Up 29-26, Wake Forest had the ball and the game. William Pfeifer moved it from his own 25 to the Alabama 31, hitting Daniel Rosa for 12, David Wolfe for 8 and Lloyd Padilla for 18. Then Richard Allen sacked him for a 6-yard loss, and on 3rd-and-15, Michael Neil coughed it up. M. Nieto recovered for Alabama with 2:26 to go.

Axel Mcgriff went to work from his own 36. He found Larry Shields for 14 yards on 3rd-and-3, spiked it at 1:19, and moved Alabama to the Wake Forest 33. Facing 4th-and-1, Alabama sent out Tovar, who had already hit from 57 yards before halftime. The 50-yarder was no good. Wake Forest knelt twice, and that was that.

Tovar made a 57-yarder at the end of the first half. The one that mattered, from 50, missed.

How Alabama Got Back In It

Wake Forest led 14-3 after Pfeifer's 3-yard touchdown pass to Neil with 55 seconds left in the half. Then Alabama found its big plays. Shields caught a 35-yarder to set up Tovar's 57-yard field goal at 0:04. In the third, Mcgriff hit running back Edwin Neal for a 28-yard touchdown (the two-point try failed). A 59-yard punt return by A. Thomas set up a 23-yard touchdown pass to Pierre Steele. That made it 19-14 Alabama with 2:15 left in the third: 16 straight points.

Wake Forest answered right away. Wolfe brought the kickoff out of the end zone, Elias Bollinger ran for 33, and Pfeifer flipped a 1-yard touchdown pass to Wolfe with 15 seconds left in the quarter. The two-point conversion made it 22-19. Neil's 7-yard run with 7:32 left made it 29-19, and Wake Forest looked safe until Mcgriff hit Neal for a 66-yard touchdown on the very next possession.

Pfeifer: 22 of 27

W. Pfeifer · 22/27 · 247 yds · 2 TD · 1 INT

Pfeifer completed 81 percent of his passes and spread the ball around. Padilla was the go-to target with 7 catches for 134 yards, including a 37-yarder on 3rd-and-9 from the Wake Forest 8 that started the opening touchdown drive. Neil scored twice, once through the air and once on the ground, which almost makes up for the fumble. Wake Forest went 6 of 10 on third down, Alabama 3 of 10, and Wake Forest held the ball for 32:21.

Alabama: Big Plays, Not Much Else

A. Mcgriff · 14/24 · 294 yds · 3 TD · 1 INT

Mcgriff averaged 21 yards per completion, and Neal turned 3 catches into 117 yards and 2 touchdowns. But Alabama ran 28 times for 71 yards and managed just 11 first downs. Wake Forest sacked Mcgriff three times, including a 12-yard drop by safety Eli Hathaway, who finished with 9 tackles. Graig London intercepted Mcgriff in Wake Forest territory in the third quarter. Linebacker Roger Owens led Alabama with 12 tackles.
