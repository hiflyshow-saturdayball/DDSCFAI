Game Recap · Week 1

No. 1 Ohio State Runs Through the Rain at South Alabama, 37-16

235 rushing yards, a punt-return touchdown and a 17-point second quarter. The Buckeyes never trailed, but Jeffrey Herron made them work.

By Saturday Desk staffWeek 1 · 2026 season

The top team in the country went on the road in Week 1, into 83 degrees, rain and a 13 mph wind. It answered the conditions the old-fashioned way: 46 carries, 235 yards on the ground and 34 minutes of possession. Ohio State led from its first drive to the final whistle and stayed No. 1 in the new poll.

The Turning Point: 7:51 in the Second Quarter

South Alabama was hanging around at 10-6 when Allan Pagan broke loose. The running back took a handoff from Luis Davison and went 58 yards for a touchdown at 10:48 of the second. That was the first of 17 points in under eight minutes. Terrell Stoner added a 17-yard field goal, then Harold Magana took a punt 53 yards down the right sideline, broke a tackle and scored to make it 27-6 at 2:57.

Pagan finished with 70 yards on just six carries. Julian Gallegos punched in two short touchdowns, from 1 yard in the first quarter and 4 yards in the fourth, and Rickey Ruby added 61 yards on 14 carries.

Rain game, road game, Week 1. Ohio State ran it 46 times and never needed a touchdown pass.

Davison: Efficient, Not Flashy

L. Davison · 15/21 · 206 yds · 0 TD · 0 INT

The quarterback didn't throw a touchdown, and he didn't have to. Davison kept the chains moving: Ohio State converted 8 of 13 third downs and didn't turn the ball over once. John Owens was his favorite target, with four catches for 82 yards, including grabs of 27 and 26. Robert Coronado added four catches for 51 yards.

The Problem: Jeffrey Herron

J. Herron · 16/19 · 199 yds · 1 TD  |  23 car · 137 yds

South Alabama's quarterback was the best player on the field for long stretches. Herron completed 84 percent of his throws, ran for 137 yards, and accounted for 336 of his team's 355 total yards. His 10-yard touchdown pass to George Albers in the third cut the deficit to 30-16. The Jaguars also got three field goals from Lyle Oliveira, including a 51-yarder.

The Ohio State defense bent but rarely broke. It sacked Herron four times (Steven Luis, Anthony Larkin, Michael Santana and Jerome Posey), and Posey led the team with 10 tackles. On South Alabama's side, safety Ira Eaton made 15 tackles.
