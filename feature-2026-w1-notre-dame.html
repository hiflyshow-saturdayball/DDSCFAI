Fourteen Sacks, Two Failed Conversions: No. 2 Notre Dame Holds Off No. 19 Oregon, 27-25

Charles Renner had five of Notre Dame's 14 sacks, Oregon came up empty on both two-point tries, and the Irish recovered the onside kick with 9 seconds left.

Oregon finished with more yards than Notre Dame, 326 to 301. It never led. The Oregon offense was all or nothing: seven completions for 241 yards, three touchdown passes of 19 yards or longer, and almost nothing in between. The Irish sacked David Vega 14 times, held Oregon to six first downs and kept the ball for 40:54. The two points that separated them were the two Oregon conversions that failed.

The Margin: Two Tries for Two

Notre Dame led 17-6 at halftime. Efrain Callender scored on an 8-yard run with 1:06 left in the second quarter, and Wayne Keeton's 3-yard touchdown pass to Victor Cronin had come right after Leonardo Kish's strip-sack of Vega in the final seconds of the first quarter.

Then Oregon found its big plays. Vega hit David Billiot for a 93-yard touchdown with 9:24 left in the third, but Kish sacked him on the two-point try, so it was 17-12. Timothy Lessard's 43-yard field goal made it 20-12, and Vega answered with a 19-yard touchdown pass to David Gantt on third-and-18. The two-point try failed again: 20-18 instead of a tie.

Oregon scored three touchdowns and lost by two. Both two-point conversions failed.

Renner's Fourth Quarter

C. Renner · 8 tkl · 5 sacks

Keeton's 11-yard touchdown pass to James Ibarra on third-and-1 with 10:58 left made it 27-18. From there, the defensive end took over. Renner sacked Vega deep in Oregon territory with 4:54 left, and he had two more on the final drive: one at the Notre Dame 41 with 1:14 to go and one with 48 seconds left that left Oregon facing fourth-and-16.

Vega found Daniel Daley for a 48-yard touchdown on that fourth down with 37 seconds left, 27-25. Mark Peck's onside kick went 14 yards and W. Wyant recovered it for Notre Dame. One kneel-down later, it was over. Notre Dame had given Oregon that last chance by punting on fourth-and-1 from its own 49 with 2:32 left.

Notre Dame Ground It Out

W. Keeton · 14/23 · 106 yds · 2 TD · 0 INT

Keeton threw for only 106 yards, and Notre Dame didn't need more. The Irish ran 53 times for 195 yards, with Callender (18 carries, 73 yards) and Kevin Feliciano (16 for 63) splitting the work, and converted 10 of 19 third downs. Oregon went 3 of 13. Linebacker Sidney Shelton made 12 tackles and a sack for Oregon, Joshua Oden added 11, and punter A. Baker kept Oregon in it with seven punts that averaged nearly 59 yards, three of them 63 yards or longer.
